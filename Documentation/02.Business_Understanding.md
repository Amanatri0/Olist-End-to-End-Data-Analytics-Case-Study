# Business Understanding

## Company Overview

Olist is a Brazilian e-commerce marketplace that connects small and medium-sized sellers with customers through multiple online sales channels.

Instead of selling products directly, Olist acts as a marketplace platform that enables sellers to reach customers while managing payments, logistics integration, and customer experience.

The dataset represents real transactional data collected from Olist's marketplace operations between 2016 and 2018.

## KPIs

### Sales KPIs

- Total Revenue
- Orders
- Average Order Value

### Customer KPIs

- Active Customers
- Repeat Customers

### Seller KPIs

- Active Sellers
- Orders per Seller

### Delivery KPIs

- Average Delivery Time
- Late Delivery %

### Customer KPIs

- Average Review Score
- 5-Star Review %

## Assumptions

- Dataset accurately represents marketplace activity.
- Customer review scores reflect overall customer satisfaction.
- Missing values represent unavailable information rather than system errors.
- Postal codes are treated as identifiers.

## Limitations

- Dataset covers only Brazil.
- Historical dataset (2016 - 2018).
